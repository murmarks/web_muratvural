3.6 Coaching & Counseling Skills for Managers

Being a Manager Is Not Enough — You Must Be a Leader

Going beyond being a manager — guiding teams to think.

A team follows someone who guides them, not someone who merely manages them.

Leadership & Management Training enables managers to move away from the classic command-and-control mindset toward a coaching-based, participatory, and sustainable leadership culture.

The essence of this program is:

"A true leader is someone who can keep their team functioning even in their absence."

The goal is for managers to become not just decision-makers, but coach-leaders who guide their team to think, create solutions, and take responsibility.

PROGRAM PHILOSOPHY: NOT TO MANAGE, BUT TO TRANSFORM

For years, in sales and marketing organizations established across different countries, the systems built under your leadership had one goal: for the system to sustain itself even when you were not there.

This training is built precisely on that principle.

Participants learn how to execute their management duties with a coaching approach:

  • Asking questions instead of giving orders,
  • Creating awareness instead of judging,
  • Guiding instead of instructing,
  • Trusting instead of controlling.

This transformation turns the manager not just into a more effective one, but into a leader who transforms their team into a self-directing structure.

PROGRAM STRUCTURE: FOUR LAYERS OF LEADERSHIP

1. Self-Management – The Foundation of Leadership

Before managing others, one learns to manage their own behavior, communication style, and emotions.

Self-awareness, the backbone of leadership, is built at this stage.

2. Team Management – Leadership with a Coaching Approach

Participants learn to use "coaching language" in communicating with their teams: asking open-ended questions, active listening, facilitating solutions, techniques to turn feedback into a tool for development rather than a threat.

3. Performance Management – Trust-Based Accountability

Performance monitoring is taken out of the punishment-reward system and handled within the framework of a sense of responsibility and mutual trust.

The leader adopts the approach of "I'm not criticizing you for what you didn't do,

I'm asking how you can do better."

4. Strategic Leadership – Building a Sustainable Organizational Culture

Managers learn to shape not only today's decisions, but also tomorrow's team structure.

By transferring the coaching culture to their own teams, they create a cascading development model.

TRAINING METHOD: LEADERSHIP IS A TESTABLE COMPETENCY

The program is conducted through real management scenarios rather than theoretical leadership concepts.

Participants bring examples from their own teams, and work one-on-one on meetings, performance issues, and conflict cases.

This method transforms abstract "leadership principles" into measurable skills: communication, guidance, empathy, structured feedback, crisis management.

The training includes interactive workshops, case analyses, and "coaching simulations."

The Contribution of Real Experience

The difference of this program is not in leadership theories, but in being fed by real experiences with hundreds of managers over the years.

The transformation of teams established in different countries into self-operating structures proved how sustainable this approach is.

The systems you shaped over the years continued to produce even when the leader was absent — because the leaders were coaching leaders.

The knowledge distilled from these experiences is now being transferred through training: each participant gains a perspective to manage their own team with the same autonomy.

What Is Provided

  • Leadership style analysis and development plan
  • Coaching-based management skills
  • Performance interview and feedback techniques
  • Motivation, empathy, and responsibility development methods
  • Steps to build a trust culture within the team
  • Strategic leadership and sustainable organizational management

Who Is It For

  • Sales and marketing directors
  • Managers with regional or country responsibility
  • Newly promoted leaders
  • Organizations seeking to institutionalize a coaching culture

True Leadership Is Leaving Behind a System That Functions Even in Your Absence

Leadership is not just about guiding people,
it is about creating a team that does not need guidance.
This training takes managers to that point:
each of them becomes the coach, mentor, and strategic guide of their own team.
The result:
Not a staff of managers,
but an organization that builds a chain of leaders.
